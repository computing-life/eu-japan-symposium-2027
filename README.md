# Computing Life: Supercomputing and AI for Biology
**A conversation between Europe and Japan empowered by agentic research**
Website for the Europe–Japan symposium on AI and HPC for the life sciences, covering genomics, protein design, cell simulation and drug discovery. Organised by IPR Osaka University, RIKEN, JST FOREST and BSC, co-hosted by JST ARiSE and supported by EuroHPC HANAMI.
